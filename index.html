Projects

Car Damage Detection

Computer Vision • OBD/ECU Integration • Report Generation • Repair shop locator

Project overview: This Car Damage Detection project is an advanced solution designed to identify both external and internal vehicle damage using cutting-edge technology. The system analyzes images and real-time video feeds to detect external issues such as dents, scratches, and broken parts. For internal diagnostics, it leverages data from the car’s ECU logs and OBD (On-Board Diagnostics) in real time to identify engine or structural problems.

In addition to damage detection, the system automatically generates a comprehensive report detailing the identified issues. It also locates the nearest repair shops, providing users with convenient options for immediate assistance. This integrated approach ensures quick, accurate damage assessment and streamlines the repair process — ideal for car owners, insurance companies, and automotive service providers.

Technologies: YOLO (v8), OpenCV, Flask, Python, OBD-II, MQTT/WebSockets, SQLite/Postgres, HTML/CSS/JS

Smart IoT solution using rain sensors + microcontroller to automatically protect clothes/vehicle in rain.

Developed an IoT-Based Automatic Rain Shield System to protect clothes from unexpected rainfall. Demonstrated skills in IoT integration, automation, and real-time sensor-based control.
